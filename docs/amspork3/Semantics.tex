These are small-step operational semantics.
We apply rewrite rules to $\langle \sporks, \sporksigma, \sporktr^* \rangle$
(statement, value environment, trace event sequence) until we reach the form
$\langle \texttt{pass}, \sporksigma, \sporktr^* \rangle$.

The initial statement $\sporks$ is the body of the called Exo-GPU procedure ($\sporkp$),
the initial $\sporksigma$ is provided by the outside caller of $\sporkp$,
and the initial $\sporktr^*$ is empty.
A program that gets \emph{stuck} (no rule applies before reaching \texttt{pass}) is wrong.
This is how we express failed assertions, ill-typed operations such as \texttt{True + 1},
accesses outside $\textrm{dom}(\sporksigma)$, and reads of uninitialized ($\bot$) data;
we do not otherwise spell out type checking or bounds checking.
For programs with unsafe parallelization, the ``checked trace concatenation operator''
$\sporkConcatChk$ (Section~\ref{sec:TraceChecks}) serves a similar purpose.
By Section~\ref{sec:SyncEnvEquiv}, we can replace the intially-empty $\sporktr^*$
with $\sporkenv = \varnothing$ and replace $\sporkConcatChk$ with $\sporkConcatEnv$
without affecting whether a program gets stuck.

For brevity and to highlight which rules can or cannot yield a changed $\sporksigma$ or $\sporktr^*$,
small step reductions $\langle \text{``before''} \rangle \to \langle \text{``after''} \rangle$
are abbreviated as $\sporkRule{\text{``common''}}$, where ``common'' contains one or more $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ pairs.
\emph{Syntactically substituting} $B$ for $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ inside ``common'' yields ``before'',
and syntactically substituting $A$ for $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ inside ``common'' yields ``after''.

The $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ operator is on the meta-language of rules and has no Exo-GPU operator precedence.

The presented semantics are the \emph{parallel semantics};
for brevity, we do not repeat everything to specify sequential semantics (Section~\ref{sec:LanguageOverview}).
The rules for sequential semantics are specified by deleting traces from the rules,
disregarding annotations $\sporkwinAnn,\sporktidMap$
(thus eliminating the need to run collective analysis prior to sequential interpretation),
and deleting any resulting no-op rules.
This is always well-defined, as the output $\sporks, \sporksigma$ has no dependence on the trace or deleted annotations.
