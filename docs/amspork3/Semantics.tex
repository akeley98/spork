These are small-step operational semantics.
We apply rewrite rules to $\langle \sporks, \sporksigma, \sporktr^* \rangle$
(statement, value environment, trace event sequence) until we reach the form
$\langle \texttt{pass}, \sporksigma, \sporktr^* \rangle$.
The initial $\sporksigma$ is provided by the outside caller of the Exo-GPU $\sporkproc$
and the initial $\sporktr^*$ is empty.

For brevity and to highlight which rules can or cannot yield a changed $\sporksigma$ or $\sporktr^*$,
small step reductions $\langle \text{``before''} \rangle \to \langle \text{``after''} \rangle$
are abbreviated as $\sporkRule{\text{``common''}}$, where ``common'' contains one or more $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ pairs.
\emph{Syntactically substituting} $B$ for $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ inside ``common'' yields ``before'',
and syntactically substituting $A$ for $\sporkSub{B}{A}$ inside ``common'' yields ``after''.
