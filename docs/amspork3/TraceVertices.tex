\magicSubsection{Trace Vertices}{sec:TraceVertices}

Each vertex $\sporkvert: \sporkVert$ is an integer/trace-event pair $(\sporkz,\sporktr)$.
$\sporktrV: \sporkTr^* \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkVert)$ converts a trace (sequence)
into a set of vertices by pairing each trace event with its 0-based index in the sequence.
\begin{align}
    \sporktrV(()) &= \varnothing \\
    \sporktrV(\sporktr^* \sporkConcatRaw \sporktr_0) &= \sporktrV(\sporktr^*) \cup \{ (\abs{\sporktr^*}, \sporktr_0 ) \}
    \label{eq:trV}
\end{align}
Each trace event $\sporktr$ advertises two kinds of inbound and outbound sets:
\textbf{(a)} observer sets\footnote{Legacy: ``visibility set''}
$\sporkObIn(\sporktr)$ and $\sporkObOut(\sporktr)$,
where an observer $\sporkob: \sporkOb$ is a pair $(\sporktid, \sporkqtl)$;
and, \textbf{(b)} pending await sets $\sporkAwIn(\sporktr)$ and $\sporkAwOut(\sporktr)$.
They are defined per-trace-event-constructor in Figure~\ref{fig:InOutSets}.

For reasons that will become clearer in Section~\ref{sec:SyncEnv} (Synchronization Environment),
we define querying the ``environment effect'' (read/mutate tag at some $x[z_i^*]$) of $\sporkTrPostAccess$ vertices.
We can also query the qualitative timeline mask of such vertices.
With $\sporkvPost: \sporkVPost$ (subset of $\sporkVert$) denoting vertices $(\sporkz, \sporktr)$ where $\sporktr$ is constructed with $\sporkTrPostAccess$,
\begin{align}
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkenvEff: \sporkEnvEff \Coloneqq (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*) & \\
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkvEff: \sporkVPost \to \sporkEnvEff \notag \\
& \sporkvEff((\sporkz, \sporkTrPostAccess(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*, \_, \_, \_, \_, \_))) = (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*)
\label{eq:vEff} \\
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkvMask: \sporkVPost \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkQtl) \notag \\
& \sporkvMask((\sporkz, \sporkTrPostAccess(\_, \_, \_, \_, \_, \_, \sporkQtlMask, \_))) = \sporkQtlMask
\label{eq:vMask}
\end{align}
