\magicSubsection{Trace Vertices}{sec:TraceVertices}

Each vertex $\sporkvert: \sporkVert$ is an integer/trace-event pair $(\sporkz,\sporktr)$.
$\sporktrVerts: \sporkTr^* \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkVert)$ converts a trace (sequence)
into a set of vertices by pairing each trace event with its 0-based index in the sequence.
\begin{align}
    \sporktrVerts(()) &= \varnothing \\
    \sporktrVerts(\sporktr^* \sporkConcatRaw \sporktr_0) &= \sporktrVerts(\sporktr^*) \cup \{ (\abs{\sporktr^*}, \sporktr_0 ) \}
    \label{eq:sporktrVerts}
\end{align}
Each trace event $\sporktr$ advertises two kinds of first and second sets:
\textbf{(a)} timeline signature sets\footnote{Legacy: ``visibility set''}
$\sporkTlSigA(\sporktr)$ and $\sporkTlSigB(\sporktr)$,
where a timeline signature $\sporktlSig: \sporkTlSig$ is a pair $(\sporktid, \sporkqtl)$;
and, \textbf{(b)} pending await sets $\sporkPendA(\sporktr)$ and $\sporkPendB(\sporktr)$.
They are defined per-trace-event-constructor in Figure~\ref{fig:VertexTable}.

For reasons that will become clearer in Section~\ref{sec:SyncEnv} (Synchronization Environment),
we define the ``environment key'' (read/mutate tag at some array element) of $\sporkTrNewAccess$ vertices.
The environment key and qualitative timeline mask can be queried from such vertices.
With $\sporkNewVert$ (subset of $\sporkVert$) denoting vertices $(\sporkz, \sporktr)$ where $\sporktr$ is constructed with $\sporkTrNewAccess$,
\begin{align}
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkenvKey: \sporkEnvKey \Coloneqq (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*) & \\
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkvertKey: \sporkNewVert \to \sporkEnvKey \notag \\
& \sporkvertKey((\sporkz, \sporkTrNewAccess(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*, \_, \_, \_, \_, \_))) = (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*)
\label{eq:vertKey} \\
{\color{gray}\bullet\,}
& \sporkvertMask: \sporkNewVert \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkQtl) \notag \\
& \sporkvertMask((\sporkz, \sporkTrNewAccess(\_, \_, \_, \_, \_, \_, \sporkQtlMask, \_))) = \sporkQtlMask
\label{eq:vertMask}
\end{align}
