\magicSubsection{Trace Concatenation Check}{sec:TraceConcatChk}

The checked concatenation $\sporktr^* \sporkConcatChk \sporktr_d$
can fail only for $\sporktr_d$ constructed from the $\sporkTrPreAccess$ constructor.
The concatenated ``destination'' event is legal if and only if the event is reachable
from \textbf{all} prior ``source'' $\sporkTrPostAccess$ events to the same array element (same $\sporkx$ and $\sporkz_i^*$),
excluding source/destination pairs both tagged as $\sporkread$.
The order of these analyzed paths ($\sporkTrPostAccess\to\sporkTrPreAccess$)
is opposite to the order $\sporktrAccessHelper$ adds trace events,
so we don't analyze paths within a single access only.

The reachability depends on edges filtered by the $\sporkQtlMask$ of the source event.

With $\sporkPATH$ denoting the typical path-existence decision problem (accepts 0-length paths and ignores edge labels),
define helpers $\sporktrReach$, $\sporktrVPost$, $\sporkmutReqs$:
\begin{align}
    {\color{gray}\bullet\,} &
    \sporktrReach: \sporkTr^* \times \sporkVPost \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkVert)
    \codecomment{\,(Vertices with path from given source $\sporkTrPostAccess$ vertex)}
    \notag \\
    & \sporktrReach(\sporktr^*, \sporkvPost[s]) = \{
        \sporkvert_d \in \sporkVert_G \mid \sporkPATH(\sporkVert_G, \sporkEdge_G, \sporkvPost[s], \sporkvert_d) \} \label{eq:trReach} \\
    & \text{where } \sporkVert_G = \sporktrV(\sporktr^*), \sporkEdge_G = \sporktrE(\sporktr^*, \sporkvMask(\sporkvPost[s])) \notag \\
    %% & \quad\mid
    %%     \sporkPATH(\sporktrV(\sporktr^*), \sporktrE(\sporktr^*, \textsf{maskOf}(\sporkvert_s)), \sporkvert_s, \sporkvert_d)
    %% \} \label{eq:trReach} \\
    {\color{gray}\bullet\,} &
    \sporktrVPost: \sporkTr^* \times \mathcal{P}(\sporkEnvEff) \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkVPost) \notag \\
    & \sporktrVPost(\sporktr^*, \sporkEnvEff_a) = \{
    \codecomment{\,($\sporkTrPostAccess$-constructed vertices, filtered by $(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*)$)}
    \notag \\
    & \quad (\sporkz, \sporkTrPostAccess(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*, \_, \_, \_, \_, \_))
      \in \sporktrV(\sporktr^*)
      \mid (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*) \in \sporkEnvEff_a \}
    \label{eq:trVPost} \\
    {\color{gray}\bullet\,} & \sporkmutReqs: \sporkMut \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkMut)
    \codecomment{\,(Will be used to ignore read/read pairs)} \notag \\
    & \sporkmutReqs(\sporkread) = \{\sporkmutate\} \quad \sporkmutReqs(\sporkmutate) = \{ \sporkread, \sporkmutate \}
    \label{eq:mutReqs}
\end{align}
For $\sporktr_d = \sporkTrPreAccess(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*, \sporkTidPre, \sporkQtlPre, \sporkAwFree)$,
\begin{align}
    & \sporktr^* \sporkConcatChk \sporktr_d = \begin{cases}
        \sporktr^* \sporkConcatRaw \sporktr_d & \textsf{isLegal} \\
        \emph{undefined} & \text{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
    \label{eq:concatChk} \\
    \text{where \textsf{isLegal} = }
    & \textbf{let } \sporktr^*_\textrm{cat} = \sporktr^* \sporkConcatRaw \sporktr_d \textbf{ in} \notag \\
    \codecomment{(newly added vertex)\quad}
    & \textbf{let } \sporkvert_d = (\abs{\sporktr^*}, \sporktr_d) \textbf{ in} \notag \\
    \codecomment{(mutates and maybe reads at $\sporkx[\sporkz_i^*]$)\quad}
    & \textbf{let } \sporkEnvEff_a = \sporkmutReqs(\sporkmut) \times \{ \sporkx \} \times \{ \sporkz_i^* \} \textbf{ in} \notag \\
    \codecomment{(all prior matching $\sporkTrPostAccess$...\quad}
    & \textbf{let } \sporkVPost[s] = \sporktrVPost(\sporktr^*_\textrm{cat}, \sporkEnvEff_a) \textbf{ in} \notag \\
    \codecomment{...must have a path to $\sporkvert_d$)\quad}
    & \forall \sporkvPost[s] \in \sporkVPost[s].\quad
    \sporkvert_d \in \sporktrReach(\sporktr^*_\textrm{cat}, \sporkvPost[s])
\end{align}
$\sporktr^* \sporkConcatChk \sporktr_d = \sporktr^* \sporkConcatRaw \sporktr_d$ for all other $\sporktr_d$.
