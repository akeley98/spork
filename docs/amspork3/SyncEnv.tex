\sporkSection{Synchronization Environment}{sec:SyncEnv}

Raw traces are far too cumbersome to analyze directly,
especially given the all-to-all checking required by $\sporkConcatChk$.
Just as a trace like $x \leftarrow 3; y \leftarrow 4; x \leftarrow x + 10; y \leftarrow y + 20$
may be compressed to a value environment $\{(x, 13), (y, 24)\}$,
discarding the specific history of how $x$ and $y$ received their values
in favor of just the final result,
we would also like to compress our traces into a \emph{synchronization environment}.

This raises the question of what the ``final value'' of a $\sporkTrPostAccess$ vertex $\sporkvPost$ is.
$\sporkvPost$ may be summarized by considering all vertices reachable from
$\sporkvPost$ and taking the union of all such vertices' outbound sets
(Figure~\ref{fig:EnvVisGrows}).
Then, whether a new vertex $\sporkvert$ is reachable from $\sporkvPost$ is immediately known
from testing for intersection between $\sporkvert$'s inbound sets and said union.
This correspondence is $\sporktoEnvTotal$ (Section~\ref{sec:SyncEnvTotal}),
and this union, along with the $\sporkQtlMask$ of the vertex, is a \emph{visibility record}:
\begin{align}
    \sporkenvVis: \sporkEnvVis &\Coloneqq (\sporkOb_v, \sporkAw_v, \sporkQtlMask)
\end{align}
The $\sporkTrPostAccess$ as a whole corresponds to a $(\sporkenvEff, \sporkenvVis)$ pair,
and the entire trace corresponds to a synchronization environment:
\begin{align}
    % Already defined EnvEff, but this is the proper place.
    \sporkenvEff: \sporkEnvEff &\Coloneqq (\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*) \\
    \sporkEnv &= \mathcal{P}(\sporkEnvEff \times \sporkEnvVis)
\end{align}
Unlike value environments, which store a single value for each $(\sporkx, \sporkz_i^*)$ key,
the synchronization environment needs to track \emph{all} prior reads/mutates at $\sporkx[\sporkz_i^*]$,
so we treat $\sporkenv$ as a relation $\sporkEnvEff \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkEnvVis)$:
\begin{align}
    \sporkenv(\sporkenvEff) = \{ \sporkenvVis \mid (\sporkenvEff, \sporkenvVis) \in \sporkenv \}
\end{align}
We ultimately drop the trace graph entirely in favor of $\sporkenv$
($\sporktoEnvTotal$ is only an intermediate proof step).
In this section, we'll define $\sporkenv$ and $\sporkConcatEnv$
as semantics-preserving drop-in replacements for $\sporktr^*$ and $\sporkConcatChk$;
i.e., alternative semantics (Section~\ref{sec:Semantics})
with $\sporktr^*$ and $\sporkConcatChk$ replaced with $\sporkenv$ and $\sporkConcatEnv$
will get stuck on an input program iff said program gets stuck with the original semantics.
