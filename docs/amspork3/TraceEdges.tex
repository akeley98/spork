\sporkSubsection{Trace Edges}{sec:TraceEdges}

Pairs of vertices are conditionally connected by edges into a DAG based on the overlap of the source vertex's outbound sets and destination vertex's inbound sets.
Each observer and pending await $\sporkob \in \sporkOb_e$, $\sporkaw \in \sporkAw_e$ found in the overlap is a \emph{witness} of the edge,
and an edge exists iff it has at least one witness.
The witnessing observers are further filtered by a given $\sporkQtlMask$ (Section~\ref{sec:ProxyFences}).
Edges can only go from lower-indexed vertices in the trace to higher-indexed vertices (in agreement with trace total order).

The set of graph edges $\sporktrE: \sporkTr^* \times \mathcal{P}(\sporkQtl) \to \mathcal{P}(\sporkEdge)$ is given by
\begin{align}
    & \sporkEdgeArrow{(\sporkz_1, \sporktr_1)}{(\sporkz_2, \sporktr_2)} \in \sporktrE(\sporktr^*, \sporkQtlMask) \iff & \\
    & (\sporkz_1, \sporktr_1), (\sporkz_2, \sporktr_2) \in \sporktrV(\sporktr^*) \land{} \sporkz_1 < \sporkz_2 & \sporkComment{(pick 2 vertices in trace total order)} \\
    \land{} & (\sporkOb_e \ne \varnothing \lor \sporkAw_e \ne \varnothing) &
    \sporkComment{(at least one witness)} \\
    \text{where } & \sporkOb_e = \sporkObOut(\sporktr_1) \cap \sporkObIn(\sporktr_2) \cap (\sporkTid\times\sporkQtlMask) &
    \sporkComment{(witnessing observers, filtered by $\sporkQtlMask$)} \notag \\
    & \sporkAw_e = \sporkAwOut(\sporktr_1) \cap \sporkAwIn(\sporktr_2) &
    \sporkComment{(witnessing pending awaits)} \notag
\end{align}
