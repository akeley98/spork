\magicSubsection{Trace Edges}{sec:TraceEdges}

Pairs of vertices are conditionally connected by edges into a DAG based on the overlap of their second and first sets;
each edge is labelled by the timeline signature $\sporktlSig$ or pending await $\sporkpend$ found in the overlap.
The $\sporktlSig$-labelled edges are further filtered by a given $\sporkQtlMask$.
Edges can only go from lower-indexed vertices in the trace to higher-indexed vertices.

Given $\sporkvert_1, \sporkvert_2 \in \sporktrVerts(\sporktr^*)$ and $\sporkvert_1$ is ordered before $\sporkvert_2$,
\begin{align*}
    (\sporkEdgeArrow{\sporkvert_1}{(\sporktid, \sporkqtl)}{\sporkvert_2}) \in \sporktrEdges(\sporktr^*, \sporkQtlMask)
    & \iff
    (\sporktid, \sporkqtl) \in \sporkTlSigB(\sporkvert_1) \cap \sporkTlSigA(\sporkvert_2) \land
    \sporkqtl \in \sporkQtlMask \\
    (\sporkEdgeArrow{\sporkvert_1}{\sporkpend}{\sporkvert_2}) \in \sporktrEdges(\sporktr^*, \sporkQtlMask)
    & \iff
    \sporkpend \in \sporkPendB(\sporkvert_1) \cap \sporkPendA(\sporkvert_2)
\end{align*}
