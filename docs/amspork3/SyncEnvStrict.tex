\sporkSubsection{Strictness Comparison}{sec:SyncEnvStrict}

We write $\sporkenvVis_s \sporksqVis \sporkenvVis_p$ to mean that $\sporkenvVis_s$ is at least as \emph{strict} as $\sporkenvVis_p$; define this as:
\begin{equation}
  (\sporkOb_s, \sporkAw_s, \sporkQtlMask[s]) \sporksqVis (\sporkOb_p, \sporkAw_p, \sporkQtlMask[p]) \Coloneqq  % Is this the right symbol?
  \sporkOb_s \subseteq \sporkOb_p \land
  \sporkAw_s \subseteq \sporkAw_p \land
  \sporkQtlMask[s] \subseteq \sporkQtlMask[p]
  \label{eq:sqVis}
\end{equation}
The intuition is the $\sporkConcatEnv\sporkTrPreAccess$ check (equation~\ref{eq:envConcatPre}) cannot have the $\sporksyncsWith$ requirement fail for $\sporkenvVis_p$ if it already cannot fail for the subset $\sporkenvVis_s$ since the requirement is entirely based on non-empty intersection tests.

Similarly, $\sporkenv_s \sporksqEnv \sporkenv_p$ means $\sporkenv_s$ is at least as strict as $\sporkenv_p$.
Define this as
\begin{align}
  \sporkenv_s \sporksqEnv \sporkenv_p \Coloneqq  % Is this the right symbol?
  & \forall(\sporkenvEff_p, \sporkenvVis_p) \in \sporkenv_p.\,
    \exists (\sporkenvEff_s, \sporkenvVis_s) \in \sporkenv_s. \notag \\
  & \sporkenvEff_s = \sporkenvEff_p \land \sporkenvVis_s \sporksqVis \sporkenvVis_p
  \label{eq:sqEnv}
\end{align}
We note that $\sporkenv_p \subseteq \sporkenv_s$ trivially implies $\sporkenv_s \sporksqEnv \sporkenv_p$ ($p,s$ are transposed!), and that $\varnothing$ is the most permissive (i.e. least strict) $\sporkenv$ possible.
