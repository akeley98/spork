\magicSubsection{Lemma 2}{sec:SyncEnvEquiv}

Similar to $\sporktoEnv$, define
\begin{align}
  & \sporktoChk: \sporkTr^* \rightharpoonup \sporkEnv \notag \\
  & \sporktoChk((\sporktr_1, ..., \sporktr_n)) = () \sporkConcatChk \sporktr_1 \sporkConcatChk ... \sporkConcatChk \sporktr_n
  \label{eq:toChk}
\end{align}
Claim
\begin{align}
  \textrm{dom}(\sporktoChk) = \textrm{dom}(\sporktoEnv)
\end{align}
Presumably proven by induction on the trace length.
