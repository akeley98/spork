\sporkSubsection{Equivalence with Trace Checking}{sec:SyncEnvEquiv}

\sporkMainKey{Theorem:}
$() \sporkConcatChk \sporktr^*$ is defined iff $\varnothing \sporkConcatEnv \sporktr^*$ is defined.

\emph{Proof} by induction on $\abs{\sporktr^*}$.
Base: both are defined for $\sporktr^* = ()$.
Step: if either fold over $\sporktr^*$ is undefined, then by induction both are, and so are both folds over $\sporktr^*_\mathrm{cat}$.
Otherwise $() \sporkConcatChk \sporktr^* = \sporktr^*$ (every successful $\sporkConcatChk$ is $\sporkConcatRaw$),
and $\sporkenv = \varnothing \sporkConcatEnv \sporktr^* = \sporktoEnvTotal(\sporktr^*)$ by the Closed-Form Lemma.
If $\sporktr_d$ is not constructed with $\sporkTrPreAccess$, both steps are defined.
Otherwise let $\sporktr_d = \sporkTrPreAccess(\sporkmut, \sporkx, \sporkz_i^*, \_, \_, \_)$ and $\sporkEnvEff_e = \sporkmutReqs(\sporkmut) \times \{\sporkx\} \times \{\sporkz_i^*\}$;
it remains to show that $\sporkisLegalChk$ of equation~\ref{eq:concatChk} holds iff $\sporkisLegalEnv$ of equation~\ref{eq:envConcatPre} does.

Step Lemma (a) rewrites $\sporkisLegalChk$ (equation~\ref{eq:concatChk}) as
\begin{align}
  \forall \sporkvPost \in \sporktrVPost(\sporktr^*, \sporkEnvEff_e).\; \sporksyncsWith(\sporkvVis(\sporktr^*, \sporkvPost), \sporktr_d)
\end{align}
By equation~\ref{eq:toEnvTotal}, $\sporkenv(\sporkenvEff) = \{ \sporkvVis(\sporktr^*, \sporkvPost) \mid \sporkvPost \in \sporktrVPost(\sporktr^*, \{\sporkenvEff\}) \}$,
so quantifying over $\sporkenvEff \in \sporkEnvEff_e$, then $\sporkenvVis \in \sporkenv(\sporkenvEff)$, as $\sporkisLegalEnv$ does (equation~\ref{eq:envConcatPre}),
is the same as quantifying over $\sporkvPost \in \sporktrVPost(\sporktr^*, \sporkEnvEff_e)$. $\square$
